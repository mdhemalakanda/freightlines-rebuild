Hassle-Free Door-to-Door Services with FREIGHT LINES (BD)

FREIGHT LINES (BD) offers reliable door-to-door shipping services designed to simplify your logistics process. Our service ensures that your cargo is picked up from your doorstep and delivered directly to its final destination, whether domestically or internationally.

Our Service

  1. 01

    Order Placement

    Start by contacting us to schedule a pick-up and provide details about your shipment.

  2. 02

    Pick-Up

    Our team arrives at your location to collect the goods, ensuring they are properly packed and labeled.

  3. 03

    Documentation

    We handle all necessary paperwork, including shipping documentation, invoices, and any required permits.

  4. 04

    Transportation Coordination

    Depending on the destination, we arrange for the most suitable mode of transport—air, sea, or road.

  5. 05

    Customs Clearance (Export)

    For international shipments, we manage export customs clearance, ensuring all regulations are met.

  6. 06

    In-Transit Monitoring

    Throughout the journey, we track the shipment and provide regular updates on its status.

  7. 07

    Customs Clearance (Import)

    Upon arrival in the destination country, we handle import customs clearance, navigating all local regulations.

  8. 08

    Final Delivery

    After clearing customs, we arrange for the last-mile delivery to the recipient's doorstep, ensuring safe and timely arrival.
